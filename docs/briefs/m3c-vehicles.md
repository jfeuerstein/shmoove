## What to build
**1. Geometry for all 13 ride kinds** (`RideKind` in `src/sim/lines.ts`), built with `MeshBuilder` for `inkMaterial()` (attributes `position`, `color`, optional `glow`; no normals; faces counter-clockwise from outside). Local frame: +z is the direction of travel; origin on the path (road level for ground rides; the carriage/kite/hook itself for overhead rides, i.e. the point the timetable moves). Chunky low-poly, flat inks, no textures. Each must read at a glance as what it is, and must make the grab point obvious: ground rides have a pink grab bar at `(0, anchorY, -anchorBack)`; overhead rides have a visible strap hanging from the carriage down to `(0, anchorY - hang, -anchorBack)` ending in a pink handle (the skater's hand goes exactly there: see `grabPoint`).
- `tram`: exists; keep, and add a pantograph. `ghosttram`: the same silhouette, pale (paper/cobalt), windows glowing, no destination text glow in mango: an eerie look that still takes the line's livery accent.
- `funicular`: stepped cabin whose floor stays level on a slope: three stepped compartments (it is drawn pitched with the track by the view, so step the body the other way by about 20 degrees).
- `bus`: a long night bus, big windscreen, roof luggage rack, glowing window band.
- `truck`: fish truck: cab + insulated box with a big fish emblem made of quads on both sides, crates on the tailgate.
- `forklift`: small, mast and forks at the front carrying a pallet, roll cage, beacon lamp (glow).
- `moped`: rider on a moped with a top box; the rider is a few boxes in the skater's style.
- `handcart`: a porter pushing a loaded handcart (sacks/crates), parasol on top.
- `parade`: the wedding float: a decorated flatbed with an arch, bunting, a couple on top and a three-piece band behind; clearly the most maximal thing in the city.
- `crane`: the gantry crane's trolley: a cab on rails with cables down to a big hook block; the hook is the strap end.
- `cablecar`: a gondola hanging from a grip arm, with the strap hanging below its floor.
- `kite`: a big diamond or box kite with a long tail of bows, the strap being its line with a handle; `kite` lines with `minChain > 1` (the king kite) are much bigger with a crown motif.
- `blimp`: a fat airship with fins, a gondola, the line name as blocky stripes, a long rope ladder as the strap.
**2. Liveries.** `line.livery` = `{ base, stripe, accent, pattern }`, pattern 0 band, 1 chevrons, 2 checks, 3 diagonal, 4 dots, 5 zigzag. Implement the six patterns as real geometry (quads on the flanks and roof), not just a flat stripe, so the pattern is recognisable at 100 m; put the pattern on both sides and a big roof mark (accent) visible from above and behind, since the camera usually looks down from behind. Windows and lamps carry `glow` so they light at night.
**3. Animation on twos.** Things that should move, stepped at 12 Hz from the sim tick (`Math.floor(tick / 5)`), by swapping between a few pre-built geometry variants per line (like the skater does), never by rebuilding geometry per frame: wheels/legs of the porter and rider (2 frames), parade band and bunting (2–3 frames), kite tail (3 frames), forklift beacon (2 frames), blimp fins/propeller (2 frames). Static kinds use one geometry.
**4. The view.** `createVehicleView(world)` keeps its interface. Requirements: one shared material; vehicles further than 240 m from the camera or out of service are not drawn (the view gets the camera position through a new optional method `setCamera(x, y, z)` that `view.ts` does not call yet: default to drawing everything when it was never called; the lead will wire it); blob shadows only for vehicles within 120 m; overhead rides cast their shadow on the ground or roof below (`topHeight`); pitch follows the slope for ground rides and is zero for overhead ones except the funicular rule above; no per-frame allocation in `update`.
**5. The line-up fixture** (`src/sim/lineup.ts`, `buildLineup(seed): World`): a flat 240 x 240 m plaza at y = 0 with 16 lines, one per row of the DESIGN section 5 table, using the ids, names, kinds and overhead flags from that table and liveries you design (see the rule below). Each line is a short shuttle or small loop laid out in parallel lanes 12 m apart so all 16 vehicles pass in front of the spawn in turn; overhead ones at 7–12 m with straps that a hop can reach for at least the crane and one cable car. Give each sensible body sizes, anchors, rope and hang values (these numbers are a first proposal the ride-data task will reuse, so make them playable: check a skater can actually hitch each one in a sim test). All windows `[[0, 1]]` so everything runs. Spawn at the south edge facing the lanes.
**Livery rule (DESIGN section 7):** every line has a base colour, a pattern and a roof mark readable from 100 m, and no two lines share the same base + pattern pair. Lines of one district should feel related (Docks teal/mango, Mercado pink/mango, Terraces mango/cobalt, Antenna cobalt/pink; secrets pale/ink) while staying distinguishable from each other. Export the 16 liveries as `LIVERIES: Record<string, Livery>` from `src/sim/lineup.ts` so the ride-data task can import them.

## Budgets
Per vehicle geometry: at most 700 triangles (parade float and blimp may use up to 1200). One draw call per visible vehicle plus one per shadow. No textures. Total added JS at most ~25 kB gzip.
