## Design
The player learns six things, in the order the hill presents them, each by doing it once. Nothing blocks: a player who ignores a hint keeps playing and the hint returns the next time its situation comes up. Hints are off when the `hints` setting is off, and never show during a chalk race, an errand, or while a screen is open.

| Step | Situation that shows the hint | The hint | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| carve | rolling on the ground for 1 s after the start | a thumb sliding left and right; "DRAG" | the skater has carved past ±0.4 steer in both directions |
| hop | on the ground, moving, with a ledge/stair top/rail within 12 m ahead, after `carve` | a thumb flicking up; "FLICK UP" | a `hop` event |
| grind | airborne or on the ground within 8 m of a handrail's run-in, after `hop` | the rail outlined, a flick-up thumb; "LAND ON THE RAIL" | a `grindStart` event |
| hitch | a vehicle the skater could catch is within 30 m and approaching, or standing at a stop within 20 m, after `carve` | a thumb pressed still with a ring filling; "PRESS AND HOLD STILL"; a pointer over the ride's grab bar | a `hitch` event |
| sling | hitched for 1.5 s | thumb dragging then lifting; "DRAG... THEN LET GO"; the aim arrow pulses | a `release` event whose exit speed is at least 1.15x the ride's speed (a real whip, not just letting go); a plain release shows "TRY CARVING BEHIND IT FIRST" once and counts after the second release regardless |
| ink | after `sling`, whenever hitched | "STAY ON TO THE NEXT STOP" with the next stop's name | a `segmentInked` event; then a one-time pointer to the map button: "YOUR MAP" |
After `ink`, one last ticket: "NO PUSHING. EVERYTHING THAT MOVES IS A LIFT." and onboarding is complete. Chains are introduced later, the first time the skater releases with another line's vehicle within 25 m: "CATCH ANOTHER BEFORE YOU LAND" (step `chain`, done on a `hitch` with `chain >= 2`); it is not required for completion.

**The reducer** (`onboarding.ts`): state `{ done: Record<step, boolean>, shown: step | '', complete: boolean, ... }`; decides each tick which hint (if any) applies from sim state, events and the world (handrail run-ins from `world.rails` with kind 'handrail'; catchable vehicles by scanning lines near the skater with `vehicleAt`, at most every 10 ticks); events `hintShow { step }`, `hintDone { step }`, `onboardingComplete`; command `onboardingSkip()` (the settings screen's hints toggle calls it or not: your choice, say which) and `onboardingReset()`; export/import for saves under key `onboarding`. Pure and deterministic like every reducer.

**A first launch that works.** A new game must put the lesson in front of the player without luck:
- The new-game clock is chosen so that the first ride arrives when the player does. Work out, with a no-input skater from `world.spawn` (which rolls by itself), where they meet the first ground line's stop on the way down the spine, and pick the new-game start day fraction (daytime, between 0.30 and 0.45) so that a vehicle of that line pulls into that stop within a few seconds of a typical arrival (test it for a range of arrival times: a skater who carves around for up to 20 s longer still finds a vehicle standing there or arriving within 12 s). Export it as `NEW_GAME_DAY_FRAC` from the app layer and use it wherever a new game is created; a loaded save keeps its own clock.
- If no stop lies on the natural run down from the spawn, say so: that is a finding about the city, not something to paper over.

**UI** (`src/ui/hint*.ts`): one hint at a time as a small ticket stub low on the screen, above the hold cue and clear of the skater: a thumb glyph drawn in inline SVG and animated on twos through `twos.ts` (slide, flick, press-and-fill, drag-then-lift: four tiny two- or three-frame animations), two or three words of text, and a punched hole that fills when the step is done (with `sound.ui('punch')`). It slides in and out on twos. Respect reduced motion (static glyph). `Ui.testState()` additions: `hint` (step or ''), `hintsDone` (count), `onboardingComplete`.
**World pointers** (`src/render/hintView.ts`): `createHintView(scene, world): { update(game, alpha, dt): void; dispose(): void }`: a bobbing mango chevron (on twos) over the thing the current hint is about: the handrail's run-in for `grind`, the vehicle's grab bar or strap end for `hitch` (use `grabPoint`), nothing otherwise. One draw call. `MeshBuilder` + `inkMaterial()` with `glow`.
